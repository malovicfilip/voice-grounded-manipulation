Speak a robot command

This page records locally only, for up to 15 seconds. It does not upload audio or move the robot. Allow your browser to use the microphone when asked.

Try: “Pick the red cube and place it on the blue target.”

Microphone is off.

After downloading, enter this in the WSL robot task console, adjusting the filename if your browser renamed it:

audio /mnt/c/Users/malov/Downloads/vgm-command.wav

Whisper will transcribe the audio on Brev. Review the proposed high-level skills and type yes only if correct. The viewer itself does not interpret speech. Use the console's stop command or Ctrl+C to stop execution.
